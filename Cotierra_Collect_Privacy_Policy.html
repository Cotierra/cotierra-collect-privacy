Cotierra Collect Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Cotierra AG handles information when you use Cotierra Collect to connect to compatible Cotierra devices, synchronize operational records, and transfer them to Cotierra's data collection service.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Cotierra Collect is an Android application operated by Cotierra AG, located at Hallwylstrasse 72, 8004 Zürich, Switzerland. Cotierra AG is the point of contact for questions about the processing of information through the application.

2. Information handled by the application

Depending on the features you use, Cotierra Collect may access, store, or transmit the following categories of information:

  • Device operational records: record ID, device ID, date and time, measurement name, value, and description. This information is generated or stored on the compatible Cotierra device.
  • Connection data: device ID and the name of the Wi-Fi network to which the phone is connected (SSID), used to identify the compatible device and manage local synchronization.
  • Device connection credentials: the device ID and access key provided during the connection process. The application retains them in its local application storage to authenticate the connection and the transfer of records.
  • Information accessed through the camera: when you choose to connect a device using a QR code, the application uses the camera to read the connection information contained in that code.
  • Technical information required for operation: connection status data and the results of synchronization and transfer operations needed to perform the requested functions.

3. Android permissions

The application requests Android permissions only when they are needed for its features:

  • Camera: allows the application to scan the QR code used to configure a connection to a compatible device.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices and Wi-Fi state: allow the application to identify and manage the connection to the Cotierra device on the local network.
  • Location permission on Android versions that require it: certain Android versions require this permission for an application to read the name of the Wi-Fi network (SSID). Cotierra Collect uses this access to identify the network and facilitate synchronization. The application does not use GPS to create a location history and does not need geographic coordinates for its main functionality.

You can manage or revoke permissions in Android settings. If you revoke a permission that a feature requires, the corresponding feature — for example, scanning a QR code or identifying the Wi-Fi network — may no longer be available.

4. How the information is used

The information is used to:

  • establish and maintain the connection between the phone and a compatible Cotierra device;
  • identify the device and retrieve the operational records stored on it;
  • temporarily store records in the application's local database;
  • authenticate the transfer and send records to Cotierra's service;
  • prevent duplicate transfers and keep local records synchronized with the device;
  • operate, maintain, and support Cotierra Collect's features.

5. Local storage and transmission to Cotierra's service

Cotierra Collect temporarily stores operational records in a local database on the phone. It also stores locally the credentials required to connect to the device and authenticate the transfer.

When the user initiates an upload, the application transmits pending records to Cotierra's service over an HTTPS connection. The application uses the device credentials to authenticate that operation. Communication with the compatible device takes place over the local Wi-Fi network.

After the service confirms receipt of the records, the application marks them as processed. During a subsequent synchronization, the processed records are deleted from the phone's local database, and the application asks the AAC device to delete from its SD card the records that have already been transferred. This cleanup of the phone and the SD card does not delete the copy stored in Cotierra's service.

6. Communication and disclosure to third parties

Operational records that the user chooses to transfer are sent to Cotierra's data collection service, available at collect.cotierra.com. Based on the operational information currently available, access to those records in the service is limited to Cotierra AG and personnel authorized by the company.

Cotierra AG does not sell the records or share them with other organizations for those organizations' own purposes. Cotierra Collect and its associated service do not use advertising, analytics tools, advertising tracking, or third-party crash reporting services. Information may be disclosed when necessary to comply with a legal obligation or respond to a valid request from a competent authority.

7. Data retention and deletion

On the phone, pending records are retained until they are successfully transferred. After the service confirms receipt, they are marked as processed and deleted from the local database during a subsequent synchronization. During that synchronization, the application also asks the AAC device to delete the records already transferred from its SD card.

Records received by Cotierra's service are retained indefinitely. There is currently no automatic expiration period or automatic process that deletes records from the server after transfer. Deleting a record from the AAC device's SD card or from the phone's local copy does not delete the copy retained in Cotierra's service.

To request information about data associated with a device, or to request correction or deletion of information, write to hello@cotierra.com. Cotierra will assess the request and respond in accordance with applicable law and contractual obligations. A request does not by itself automatically delete records from the server.

8. Security

Cotierra uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. Records are transferred to Cotierra's service over HTTPS, and upload operations require authentication credentials. Device credentials are stored in the application's local storage.

Communication between the phone and the AAC device takes place over the local Wi-Fi network. Users should therefore use a trusted network and keep their phones, credentials, and devices secure.

9. Accounts and intended use

Cotierra Collect does not provide an in-app process for creating personal user accounts. The connection is configured using credentials associated with the compatible device. The application is intended for authorized individuals who operate or manage Cotierra devices, not for children.

10. International transfers

Cotierra AG is located in Switzerland and operates the data collection service associated with Cotierra Collect. For additional information about the specific location where records are processed or stored, you can contact Cotierra using the details at the end of this page.

11. Changes to this policy

Cotierra may update this policy when the application, associated services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be available on this page and will state its last updated date.
